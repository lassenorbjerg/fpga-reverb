\subsection{Determining Length of Audio in Buffer}

First we will create an equation with variables representing - bit depth, sample rate, and buffer size.

Let $B$ represent the bit depth, $R$ the sample rate, and $S$ the buffer size. The length of audio in the buffer $L$ can be calculated as:
\[
L = \frac{S}{R \cdot B}
\]
